## What makes this handbook useful

Supply-chain problems do not arrive in chapter order. A late delivery may begin as a planning assumption, become a purchasing or warehouse exception, and surface later as an invoice dispute or customer complaint. This book is organized around that reality.

The opening problem finder starts with what the reader can see. Each route then provides the checks, formulas, system fields, examples and evidence needed to reach a safe next action. The same fictional sample company and transaction set appear throughout the book so that formulas, SQL, dashboards and accounting entries can be compared without changing context.

Use it as:

- a desk reference during daily operations;
- a troubleshooting guide for live business problems;
- an implementation and migration playbook;
- a study bridge from basic terminology to advanced practice;
- a reusable source of checklists, controls, formulas and technical examples.

## How to use it at work

1. Open **[FIND-01]** near the front of the book.
2. Match the visible symptom rather than guessing the cause.
3. Record one real order, item, supplier, shipment, invoice, report or job ID.
4. Follow the first route and run the example in a read-only or approved test area.
5. Replace only the marked company-specific values.
6. Reconcile the result and record the owner, decision and next check.

Company policy, signed contracts, system configuration and current law always take precedence. Safety, regulated-product, customs, sanctions, tax, accounting, bank-change, legal and irreversible production decisions require the responsible qualified specialist and documented approval.
