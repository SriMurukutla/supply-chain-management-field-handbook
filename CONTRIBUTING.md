# Contributing corrections and improvements

Thank you for helping improve the *Supply Chain Management Expert Field Handbook*.

This repository welcomes corrections that make the handbook clearer, more accurate or easier to use. Good submissions are specific and can be checked independently.

## Before opening an issue

Please check the latest release and search existing issues. For a content correction, include:

1. the edition and version;
2. the printed page number and route ID, such as `[SAP-MM]` or `[SQL-01]`;
3. the exact wording, formula, query or diagram label at issue;
4. the proposed correction;
5. why the change matters in practice;
6. an official source, standard, controlled example or reproducible calculation where applicable.

Do not include confidential company data, customer or supplier information, credentials, private system names, contract text that you do not own, or screenshots containing personal information.

## Editorial standards

Proposed changes should:

- use plain professional language;
- distinguish a verified fact from a possible cause;
- state the business grain, unit, period and assumptions for calculations;
- preserve read-only or test-first guidance for technical examples;
- avoid vendor-specific claims unless the applicable product, edition and date are clear;
- keep fictional examples fictional;
- respect trademarks, copyright and confidentiality.

## Review and attribution

Suggestions may be edited for consistency, combined with related changes or declined when they cannot be verified. By submitting material, you confirm that you have the right to share it and agree that accepted contributions may be distributed under this repository's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.

The author remains responsible for the final editorial decision and release.
